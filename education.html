GUIDE 01

Preparing land and soil

Observe the field, understand the soil, plan drainage and use suitable organic matter where appropriate.

Key points to remember
  • Consider soil type and field history.
  • Avoid unnecessary soil disturbance.
  • Use soil-test results to guide decisions.
GUIDE 02

Choosing and planting crops

Choose crops suited to the climate, soil, available resources and market needs.

Key points to remember
  • Use healthy, suitable planting material.
  • Follow locally recommended spacing.
  • Keep basic planting records.
GUIDE 03

Managing crop health

Inspect crops regularly and identify problems early before deciding on a response.

Key points to remember
  • Monitor fields routinely.
  • Identify the cause before treating.
  • Seek qualified local advice when uncertain.
GUIDE 04

Harvest and record keeping

Plan harvest and storage carefully and record production, costs and sales.

Key points to remember
  • Harvest at an appropriate maturity stage.
  • Keep produce clean and protected.
  • Record quantities and dates.
